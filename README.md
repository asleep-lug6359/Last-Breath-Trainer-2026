## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start the game and load a save before enabling movement-related options. Use the movement speed feature during exploration to cross ruined environments more quickly, then disable it before scripted sequences or transitions if movement behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect weapons, ammunition, recovery items, and other supplies normally. Activate the item quantity feature after opening or updating the inventory so supported values can be detected. Avoid changing item quantities during scripted interactions or while transferring between areas.

### World & Environment

Last Breath 最后的气息 combines side-scrolling exploration with a tense survival horror setting. Search abandoned locations, follow the story of Guan Jin's search for her sister, and use the trainer to reduce survival pressure while investigating dangerous areas and encountering different infected enemy types.

### Combat & Weapons

Enter combat normally and allow the trainer to detect Guan Jin's current weapon or combat state before enabling ammunition, reload, rapid-fire, or damage options. Infinite ammunition supports extended shooting, no reload keeps weapons ready, rapid fire increases attack frequency, and one-hit kills shortens encounters. Disable combat options during story scenes and boss transitions.
